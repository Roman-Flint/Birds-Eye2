    Get a quote

    Choose a shoot and tell me where it is. The estimate updates as you go, and nothing is booked or paid for until I’ve confirmed the details with you by email.

    1. You send a request

      Pick a shoot, add the address and choose a day that suits.

    2. I confirm by email

      I check the weather, the airspace rules for your area and the bus times, then confirm a time and price.

    3. Shoot day

      I walk over from the stop and fly the shoot, getting the angles you asked for.

    4. Delivery

      Photos and video arrive by download link. Prints take 1–2 weeks.

      I enjoy finding a good angle, planning a shoot carefully and creating clear photos and smooth video that people can use to show off their property. I’m based in Auckland and appreciate every opportunity to work with local people and businesses.
